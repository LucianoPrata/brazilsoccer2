Exchange Programs

This special program was created by Gerhard Benthin to accommodate youth soccer players from U.S.A, Mexico and around the world. This program is designed for players between the ages of 14 and 20 with a strong desire to play at the highest level of club, college and professional soccer. Brazil Soccer USA has a partnership with Fluminense Futebol Club in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. Fluminense Futebol Club is a professional soccer club that has successfully played its way to inclusion among the top Brazilian soccer clubs.

Soccer Camps

Our BSU soccer camps program focuses on the approaching way to training, including technical, tactical, physical aspects of the game, prioritizing individual player development and tactical understanding of the game. We use different methods such as: other coaches feedback about their players, physical tests (conditioning, running, strength trainings). Always trying to personalize each player necessity. BSU likes to apply the Brazilian creativity which emphasizes on the technique, creativity, and strong focus with fitness. Nowadays, soccer is a sport that requires a lot of conditioning. In BSU we always try to stimulate the realistic situations on a game. So, we have intense practices with a lot of action meanwhile we try to make it fun and enjoyable for our players. We have the Brazilian technique as I can say and the American mentality which has the high focus on conditioning and fitness of the game.

Goal Keeper

The Goalkeeper Training method is a systematic and specialized approach for the development of goalkeepers in soccer. This method focuses on enhancing the specific skills and the physical, technical, tactical, and mental characteristics necessary for the goalkeeper position. Here are the main components of the Goalkeeper Training method: The goalkeepers should learn how to deal with pressure & the responsibility into every game they play. the state of mind needs to be developed & systematic exercise. It is a privilege for those who play in this position & learn how to embrace the pressure & responsibility to play at their best. Coach Gerhard Benthin JR.
